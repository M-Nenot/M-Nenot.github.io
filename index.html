My Story

Learning fast, building faster

I started as a Data Analyst intern at HCLTech while finishing my Bachelor's in Information Technology with Data Science, learning to find the story hidden in messy data. That curiosity took me to Cyber Virtuous just a few months later as a Computer Vision Developer, a completely different discipline that I had to pick up fast, training object-detection models and building real-time inference pipelines with YOLO and MobileNet SSD.

As the company grew into Auvetra Labs, I co-founded it alongside people who believed in the same mission, making buildings smarter and more energy efficient through presence-sensing. Radar, computer vision and BLE all detect when a room is actually occupied. We've since earned DPIIT recognition, grants from MeitY Startup Hub and EDII-TN, and a place in the NVIDIA Inception Program.

Radar-based presence detection runs on embedded hardware, so as we built that line out, I moved from computer vision into embedded systems and firmware development, another new domain that I learned on the job in months rather than years. Today, as our Embedded and IoT Systems Engineer, I build the automation and edge-AI layer that ties it all together, including ESP32 firmware, AWS and MQTT, and industrial protocols for connecting to building systems.

Every step has meant walking into a domain I didn't know yet and getting comfortable in it fast, first data, then vision, then embedded systems. The goal has stayed the same, turning real-world signal into something reliable that quietly helps people, without them ever having to think about it.
